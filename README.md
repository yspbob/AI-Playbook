# AI Engineering Operating Model Playbook

A practical playbook for converting AI capability into governed engineering value: platform, context, governance, measurement, readiness, sequencing and the business case, targeted at mid-size engineering organisations. Market data is current as of August 2026 and dated in place.

## Read it

- **[The playbook](https://yspbob.github.io/AI-Playbook/AI_Engineering_Playbook.html)**, the full argument across twelve sections and two appendices
- **[Twelve Decisions deck (PDF)](https://yspbob.github.io/AI-Playbook/Twelve_Decisions.pdf)**, the executive version
- **[Business-case workbook (xlsx)](https://yspbob.github.io/AI-Playbook/AI_Business_Case_Model.xlsx)**, an illustrative model to rebuild with your own headcount and payroll
- **[Latest release](https://github.com/yspbob/AI-Playbook/releases/latest)**, versioned artefacts with release notes
